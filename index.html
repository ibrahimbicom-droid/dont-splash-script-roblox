Game Overview

About DONT SPLASH on Roblox

Understanding the mechanics, sound-based stealth physics, and atmospheric horror elements.

What is DONT SPLASH?

DONT SPLASH is a tension-filled psychological survival game on Roblox inspired by liminal poolrooms. Players navigate an expansive maze of tiled aquatic chambers where every step in the water generates acoustic ripples that can be detected by wandering hostile entities.

What Makes Gameplay Unique?

Unlike standard obstacle courses or chase games, DONT SPLASH punishes haste. Moving at normal sprint speeds while submerged produces loud splashing sounds. Success demands mastering stealth pacing, utilizing dry ledges, timing dashes between safe platforms, and deciphering environmental codes.

Why Players Seek Script Guides

Roblox developers and curious players frequently study how the game calculates velocity thresholds in water, triggers sound alerts, and manages atmospheric volumetric fog. Our guides provide transparent Lua architecture to help you understand these mechanics in Roblox Studio.

Promoting Safe, Educational Exploration

Don't Splash Script is committed to cybersecurity awareness. We provide verified, legitimate Lua scripts designed for your own Roblox Studio creations while teaching players how to recognize and avoid unsafe third-party exploits that compromise accounts.

Beginner Walkthrough

How to Use & Understand Roblox Scripts

A step-by-step educational guide on safely implementing Lua scripts in Roblox Studio.

01

1. Inspect the Lua Snippet

Carefully review the script code block. Safe Roblox scripts are always readable plain-text Lua files. Check for standard services like RunService, TweenService, or Players.

02

2. Open Roblox Studio

Launch the official Roblox Studio application on your computer. Create a new test place or open your game project in a sandboxed, safe local environment.

03

3. Insert the Appropriate Script

Depending on the script type indicated on the card, insert a LocalScript into StarterPlayerScripts or a standard Script into ServerScriptService.

04

4. Test & Fine-Tune Parameters

Click Play in Studio to simulate game physics. Observe the Output window (View > Output) to monitor debug prints, velocity numbers, and trigger reactions.

Critical Safety Notice: Identifying Unsafe Downloads

Never download executable files (.exe, .bat, .scr) or install browser extensions claiming to inject scripts into Roblox. Malicious third-party exploit hubs often bundle Trojan horses, token stealers, or adware.

Legitimate Lua scripts are strictly text. Don't Splash Script never asks you to download suspicious files, enter your account credentials, or install unauthorized software.
