Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026

At Don't Splash Script (accessible from https://dontsplashscript.com), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal and non-personal information collected and recorded by our website, how we utilize and safeguard that data, and your rights as a user.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two primary ways:

  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: When you contact us using our contact form or send us an email directly, you provide your name, email address, message subject, and inquiry details. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and never sell or rent your contact details to third parties.
  • Log Files and Technical Data: Like standard web hosts, our web servers automatically log basic non-personally identifiable technical information. This includes your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring and exit pages, date/time stamps, and approximate general geographic region (country or city level).

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Don't Splash Script uses cookies to remember user preferences, maintain session state, and enhance site navigation performance. A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by your web browser.

You have the choice to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to read our guides or copy script snippets.

3. Google Analytics (When Active)

When enabled via our site configuration, we may utilize Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand general traffic trends, such as which script guides are most popular and average visit duration. Google Analytics collects pseudonymous data and aggregates traffic statistics without directly identifying individual visitors.

You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

Don't Splash Script is configured to support third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, once approved. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

Note: We do not place misleading advertisement formats or encourage artificial clicks. Ad slots are kept clearly designated and separate from interactive utility buttons.

5. How We Use Collected Data

We use the data gathered on this site strictly to:

  • Operate, maintain, and optimize our educational gaming guides.
  • Ensure website stability, cybersecurity, and server integrity.
  • Diagnose technical bugs and improve mobile device compatibility.
  • Respond to legitimate user inquiries and support requests.
  • Prevent fraudulent activities, spam bot attacks, and security exploits.

6. Third-Party Links & External Sites

Our guides may contain links to external developer resources, official Roblox documentation, or community repositories. Once you leave our domain, our Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request copies of any personal information we retain about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You can request that we delete personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to the processing of your data under specific conditions.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

8. Children's Information Protection

Protecting the safety of children online is an absolute priority. Don't Splash Script does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that our site has unintentionally recorded personal information of a child, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such records from our logs.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our legal obligations, platform features, or advertising partners. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contacting Us About Privacy

If you have any questions or require additional information regarding our Privacy Policy or data practices, please reach out to our privacy administrator at:

Email: support@dontsplashscript.com
Contact Form: https://dontsplashscript.com/contact/
